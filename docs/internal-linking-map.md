# Internal Linking Map

Principles: descriptive, varied anchor text (no exact-match spam); every page reachable within ≤3 clicks of home; silo cross-links plus deliberate hub↔spoke↔related links. Anchors mix brand, service, city, and natural phrasing.

## Home (`/`) links out to
- Stove Repair service (canonical) · Gas Stove Repair · Electric Stove Repair · Emergency Stove Repair · Range Repair · Cooktop Repair
- Stove Repair Pricing (`/stove-repair-cost-san-mateo-ca/`)
- Service Areas (`/service-areas/`) + parent San Mateo location
- Problems hub + 3–4 top problem pages
- Contact · Brands hub

## Service pages link to
- 2–4 **related services** (e.g. gas ↔ range ↔ igniter; electric ↔ cooktop ↔ burner)
- 1–2 relevant **problem pages** (igniter ↔ clicking; electric ↔ not-heating)
- **Pricing** page + **Contact**
- **Parent San Mateo** location page + 2–3 nearby cities
- Back up to `/services/`

## Problem pages link to
- **Matching service** (primary CTA target)
- **Emergency** page where safety-relevant (gas smell, won't turn on, shutting off)
- **Pricing** + **Contact**
- 1–2 related problems

## Location pages link to
- **Parent San Mateo** page (non-parent cities)
- Stove Repair · Gas Stove Repair · Electric Stove Repair · Emergency Stove Repair
- **Contact**
- 2–3 **nearby cities** (adjacency-based, defined per city in `content/locations.ts`)

## Brand pages link to
- Core services + Contact + Brands hub

## Blog posts link to
- 1 primary **service** + 1 relevant **location** or **problem** + mid & final **CTA**

## Footer (global) links to
Home · Services (hub + all 10) · Problems (hub) · Brands (hub) · Locations (hub + key cities) · Pricing · Reviews · About · Contact · Privacy Policy · Terms

## Header (global)
Sticky. Services dropdown (all services + hub link) · Locations dropdown (key cities + hub) · Problems · Brands · Pricing · About · Contact. Persistent **Call Now** button (PhoneLink). Mobile: hamburger + sticky bottom call bar.
